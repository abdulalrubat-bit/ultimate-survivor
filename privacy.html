Privacy Policy — Ultimate Survivor

Last updated: 26 July 2026

The short version: Ultimate Survivor collects nothing, sends nothing, and needs no internet connection. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

Who we are

Ultimate Survivor is developed and published by Fateful Games, a sole developer based in Victoria, Australia. You can reach us at REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-EMAIL.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information about you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising in this version of the game.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally using your device's browser storage. This never leaves your phone and is not visible to us or anyone else. It consists of:

  • Your best run — time survived, level reached and bosses defeated
  • The number of runs you've played
  • Your control preference (joystick or drag) and sound setting
  • Your banked salvage and which permanent upgrades you own

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage from Android's app settings, permanently deletes all of it. There is no backup and no way for us to recover it.

Permissions

Ultimate Survivor requests no Android permissions. It has no access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, or any other device feature or data.

Internet access

The game runs entirely offline. It makes no network requests and does not require a connection to play.

Third parties

We share no data with anyone, because we hold none. The game contains no third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising software.

Google Play collects certain information when you download or update an app, such as your device type and country. That collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not this one, and we receive only aggregate, anonymous install statistics that cannot identify you.

Children

Ultimate Survivor is suitable for general audiences and is not directed specifically at children. Because we collect no data from anyone, we collect no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game introduces anything that collects or transmits data — advertising, for example — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top of the page will change. We'd encourage you to check back after a major update.
