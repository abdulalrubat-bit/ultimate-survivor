Privacy Policy — Ultimate Survivor

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Summary

Ultimate Survivor collects nothing, transmits nothing, and works fully offline. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising in this version of the game.

Stored on your device

The game saves your best run, number of runs played, control and sound preferences, banked salvage and permanent upgrades. This is stored locally and never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes all of it permanently.

Permissions and internet

The game requests no Android permissions and makes no network requests. It does not need a connection to play.

Third parties

We share no data with anyone, because we hold none. Google Play collects some information when you install or update an app; that is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences. As we collect no data from anyone, we collect none from children.

Changes

If a future version introduces anything that collects data, such as advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
